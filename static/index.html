GETTING STARTED

How to Use PhishGuard AI

  1. Open the Analyze URL section.
  2. Paste the complete website link into the input field.
  3. Select Analyze URL and wait for the result.
  4. Review the risk score, prediction, and detection reasons.
  5. Visit Scan History to review your previous scans.

A Legitimate result does not guarantee that a website is safe. Avoid entering passwords or payment details on links you do not trust.
